# The beat map

Fourteen narrative beats reverse-engineered from VSLs. Each answers a subconscious question the reader has before they can say it, and each is fodder for hooks, ads, emails, and curiosity content. Read down the beats to find the point of an ad. Tag an ad's lines to beats to find what is overbuilt and what is unreached.

| # | Beat | The question it answers | Sub-beats | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Speaker background and credibility | Who are you, and why should I listen to you about this? | general background · credentials · personality and perspective | "As a former yo-yo dieter myself, I know firsthand how demoralising it is to try every diet trend and end up back where you started." |
| 2 | Problem narrative | Do you actually understand what I'm going through, and how serious it is? | early warning signs · worsening · crisis point · emotional nadir | "I found myself walking on eggshells, afraid to say anything that might set her off." |
| 3 | Failed solutions | Why hasn't anything I've tried worked, and why is yours different? | common advice is wrong · common remedy makes it worse · comparisons | "Cutting carbs seems like the holy grail, but when you slash them your body goes into full panic mode." |
| 4 | Myths and mistakes | What beliefs am I relying on that are making it worse? | prevailing myths · costly mistakes | "One of the biggest myths about building wealth is that you need a high-paying job or an inheritance." |
| 5 | Unique mechanism preview (UMP) | Why were YOU able to figure out this hidden reason when other experts haven't? | discovery narrative · UMP trigger | "A landmark study uncovered a psychological trigger that makes women feel an uncontrollable pull. Dr. Hoffman calls it 'the Lovemap Loophole'." |
| 6 | UMP cause | What's REALLY causing it, and who or what is responsible? | surprising culprit · UMP adversaries | "The food industry adds AGEs to enhance flavour and shelf life, putting profits over people's health." |
| 7 | Unique mechanism elaboration | If this explains my issue, why doesn't everyone have it, and why is it worse now? | individual differences · changing landscape · unspoken truth | "In the past there weren't many emulsifiers in food. Now the amount has skyrocketed by more than 340%." |
| 8 | Self-reflection | How do I know this applies to ME? | UMP quiz | "Do you eat a lot of processed food? Cook at high temperatures? Struggle with belly fat despite eating well?" |
| 9 | UMS preview | How did you uniquely discover this solution when everyone else missed it? | UMS trigger | "After a devastating breakup I stumbled on an ancient Tibetan manuscript that revealed 'Emotional Resonance'." |
| 10 | UMS characterisation | How can you prove the solution is simple, intuitive, unique, and different? | blind / curiosity characterisation · single-step action · transformational roadmap · taboo reframe | "To trigger his response, all you need to do is whisper one specific phrase in his ear." |
| 11 | UMS tips and secrets | Can you give me value right now, before I commit? | how-tos, tips, ingredients, secrets | "One simple trick to create instant connection is to mirror their body language." |
| 12 | Creating the solution | How do I know this is credible, tested, and proven? | attempts and failures · first user success · unsolicited requests · zero-to-hero testimonials | "'I've struggled with my weight since high school… after 30 days I'm down 23 pounds' — Lisa H." |
| 13 | Momentum | Why act now instead of thinking it over? | urgency · scarcity | "We can only accept a limited number of couples to ensure personal attention." |
| 14 | Risk | What if it doesn't work, and what happens if I do nothing? | risk reversal · guarantees | "If you don't see a significant increase in 90 days, we refund everything and add a bonus session." |

The UMP trigger list, reused for the UMS at beat 9: scientific evidence, news, historical example, story, analogy, thought experiment, personal challenge, counterintuitive wisdom, paradoxical question.

## Ten beats short video forces into existence

Not on the map, present in the winning corpus:

- **Call-out**: who this is for.
- **Absolution**: "it's not your fault, it's the mechanism." Arguably the highest-leverage beat in direct response; buried inside failed solutions on the map.
- **Demonstration**: live proof in action.
- **Future pacing**: the after, shown.
- **Comparison**: versus named alternatives.
- **Offer reveal / value stack**.
- **CTA**: the map ends at risk; there is no beat for "here is literally what to do."
- **Re-hook**: the mid-roll pattern interrupt every 20 to 30 seconds; a floating beat that inserts between others.
- **Qualification / exclusivity**.
- **Belonging**: "join 40,000 women who…"

## The nine verdicts (what beats are for)

A beat is testimony; a verdict is the belief it closes. The verdict list is closed; the beat list is open forever.

1. My problem is really caused by Y. (beats 5, 6, 7)
2. Everything I tried failed because it never touched Y. (beats 3, 4)
3. Z fixes Y through this pathway. (beats 9, 10, 11)
4. Only this version of Z reaches Y. **The brand gate. No beat on the map.** Generic is dead → sourcing specifics → verification ritual.
5. Left alone, Y compounds. (beat 2, and the ratchet)
6. This messenger can be trusted. (beats 1, 12)
7. This applies to me. (beat 8)
8. I cannot lose. (beat 14)
9. Now, not later. (beat 13)

**The verdict audit.** Tag every line of an ad with the verdict it serves. Which verdicts are unreached? Does the market's awareness already cover them? Overbuilt verdicts show up as bloat (timing is the usual one: "sells out constantly"). Unreached verdicts show up as the reason it did not convert (risk is the usual one). Awareness collapses the map: each notch up collapses beats 3 to 9, because their verdicts arrive pre-closed, and running them anyway re-litigates settled questions on borrowed attention.

## The unaware-ad skeleton, with lengths

```
Hook          1–2 sentences
Payoff        50–2000 words
Story         1000–2000 words
Other angles  100–150 words
=== transition ===
Mechanism     200–300 words   problem → intermediary → mechanism
Solution      200–300 words   solution → unique solution
Product       150–300 words   what's different about yours
Offer         50–100 words    why buy now
```

Luke's short-ad skeleton: HOOK → POINT → MECHANISM → OFFER.
