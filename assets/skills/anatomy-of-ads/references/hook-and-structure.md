# Hook and structure

## Hook rules

The hook is concept plus angle compressed to the fewest, sharpest words. Structurally, a beat promoted to slot zero, usually the body's organising beat. Hooks rotate independently of the body, so test five hooks made maximally different on one body, not micro-variations.

Craft rules:

- Immediate relevance.
- Charged language: object words and action verbs with natural charge ("bullied", "whip", "dead muscle").
- An open loop, natural intrigue.
- Stakes; rubbernecking.
- Specific and tangible, but specificity that points at the point, not away from it.
- Caveman-simple. "Most language is filler, filler, filler, then a point. Be clear what the point is and let the rest be simple."
- No fragments. One sentence, not three.
- Consequence first, mechanism later.
- Germanic over Latinate ("cloth" over "fabric").
- A 1 to 3 intensity dial; always some wildcards.

Worked edit. Before: "My daughter got me this omelet, I caught myself staring at the stove barefoot without the broken glass feeling." The read: the point is hard to see, the specificity goes the wrong way. After: "My granddaughter bullied me into eating this weird Indonesian fruit to stop my neuropathy. Now, for the first time in three years, I can walk barefoot." The charged word is "bullied"; the rest is pain point and promise in plain words.

Second edit: "Laxatives whip your colon" has the association but you cannot see it. "Abuse it until it becomes a dead muscle that can't poop anymore" lands.

Patterns: weird facts ("why you wake up at 3:17am") · magic words ("the four-word text that makes him chase you") · weird methods ("the Japanese loophole that melts fat overnight") · incomplete loops.

Failure mode: hook and body diverging too far pays the clickbait tax, high click rate and dead conversion. The exception that proves the rule: a conspiracy-bodied ad opening with a discovery hook that pivots to suppression in line two.

## Unaware hooks

Four types for unaware traffic: **straddle** (curiosity plus relevance in the first line), **symptom** (a neutral reference plus a charge), **worldview** (a belief chain: tap the reservoir → explain why → show the cost → mechanism, product, offer), **story**.

Relevance has four levels of charge, from broad to sharp: existential (death, aging) → mechanism (cortisol, blood sugar) → condition (neuropathy, ED) → sensation (tingling feet, waking at 3am, brain fog at 2pm).

The onion ladder, unaware to problem-aware, one word at a time:

```
What onions do to you
What onions do to your body
What onions do to your health
What onions do to your blood sugar
What onions do to your neuropathy
```

Symptom hooks: "Is your dog eating grass?" → "That's a sign of digestive distress." "If you need coffee to wake up, your mitochondria are already 40% dead."

Process: hooks first, always. A hook bot plus a hook primer of 50 to 100 winning hooks plus the brief, pushed two or three times, then edited by hand.

## Structure: the four dials behind "format"

| Dial | What it is | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Medium | the container | static, video, carousel |
| Skeleton | beat order | timeline, specimen VSL, diagnosis-then-timeline, mechanism explainer, exposé |
| Conceit | what it pretends to be | podcast, street interview, 80s news desk, customer call, cue cards, song |
| Style | visual gloss | UGC, Pixar, claymation, greenscreen, whiteboard, mummies |

Every time someone says "new format", ask which dial. Luke's own list mixes all four: story-telling song ad (conceit + style), timeline (skeleton), UGC (style + actor), animated ingredients or villains (style), conspiracy timeline (skeleton with the adversary beat as spine), podcast (conceit), POV (conceit + style), specimen VSL (skeleton: the problem-cause beat promoted, trust carried by the instrument), 80s retro news (conceit).

A format is a beat promoted to spine. A hook is a beat promoted to slot zero. When the same beat takes both, format, concept, and hook arrive fused; that is why "conspiracy timeline" reads as one thing.

Skeletons are walks through the concept graph: timeline narrates forward node by node; mechanism explainer walks backward from symptom to root; exposé starts at the adversary and radiates out. Same graph, three orders, three different-feeling ads.

Awareness collapses skeletons. Each notch up collapses the middle beats; running them anyway re-litigates settled questions on borrowed attention. Short-ad skeleton: HOOK → POINT → MECHANISM → OFFER.

Format beats copy (the Lampropoulos principle): the same copy moved into a bookalog format has been the massive winner. When copy is stuck, change the format before the words.

## Conceit: the hinge

The conceit is the last decision about how you say something and the first about how you show it. It is the script's shape, not a separate step: a customer-call script has two voices, a cue-card script is one beat per card. Test for every creative element: does it carry the message, or just host it?
