# Standards

Your taste, written where the system always reads it. Every skill in this folder reads this file before it produces anything and grades its draft against it before you see it. `exodus update` never touches this file.

One entry per line. Dated. The raw thing you said or did sits under it in a quote, so the rule can be traced back to the moment. Entries are added by the `remember-that` skill (mid-session) and proposed by the `handoff` skill (end of session). Nothing is added without your yes.

Rungs, from the Standards Ladder: **taste** (in your head) → **note** (said once) → **rule** (written here) → **tool** (enforced in code) → **skill** (runs itself). Saying "remember that" climbs a rung. Saying the same note twice climbs it for you. Saying "make sure this never happens again" asks for a tool.

## How I give feedback

The skills read this first and behave accordingly. Change it and they change.

- Capture: inline when I say "remember that"; a proposal sweep at the end of every session; a review read every 10 sessions or when I ask.
- The AI may propose entries from my edits and my picks. It never writes one without a yes.
- Winning lines (a hook, a headline, a body I approved) push to the matching Exodus bank automatically. Style rules that would change a workflow or a primer are offered, not pushed.
- Files I have hand-edited are read-only to the system.
- Sections in this file split into their own files under `standards/` when they pass a page. The skill does it and tells me where things moved.
